# Crop Guide for Indian Farmers

## Kharif Crops (June–October, Monsoon Season)

### Rice (Chawal / Dhan)
- Best soil: Clayey loam, well-irrigated fields
- NPK requirement: 120-60-40 kg/hectare
- Regions: Punjab, Haryana, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh
- Sowing: June–July | Harvesting: October–November
- Key tip: Maintain 5 cm standing water during tillering stage

### Cotton (Kapas)
- Best soil: Black cotton soil (Regur)
- NPK requirement: 80-40-40 kg/hectare
- Regions: Gujarat, Maharashtra (Vidarbha), Telangana, Madhya Pradesh
- Sowing: April–May | Harvesting: October–December
- Key tip: Use Bt cotton varieties for bollworm resistance

### Soybean
- Best soil: Well-drained loamy soil
- NPK requirement: 30-60-40 kg/hectare (fixes its own nitrogen)
- Regions: Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan
- Sowing: June–July | Harvesting: September–October
- Key tip: Seed treatment with Rhizobium culture improves nitrogen fixation

### Maize (Makka)
- Best soil: Sandy loam to loamy soil
- NPK requirement: 120-60-40 kg/hectare
- Regions: Karnataka, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan
- Sowing: June–July | Harvesting: September–October
- Key tip: Apply zinc sulfate at 25 kg/hectare if zinc-deficient soil

### Bajra (Pearl Millet)
- Best soil: Sandy to sandy loam (drought resistant)
- NPK requirement: 60-30-0 kg/hectare
- Regions: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, Maharashtra
- Sowing: June–July | Harvesting: September–October
- Key tip: Excellent crop for low-rainfall and arid regions

## Rabi Crops (October–March, Winter Season)

### Wheat (Gehu)
- Best soil: Well-drained loamy to clayey loam
- NPK requirement: 120-60-40 kg/hectare
- Regions: Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh
- Sowing: October–November | Harvesting: March–April
- Key tip: First irrigation (crown root stage) at 21 days after sowing is critical

### Mustard (Sarson)
- Best soil: Loamy to sandy loam
- NPK requirement: 60-40-20 kg/hectare
- Regions: Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Haryana
- Sowing: October | Harvesting: February–March
- Key tip: Spray water-soluble boron at flowering for better pod setting

### Chickpea (Chana)
- Best soil: Well-drained loamy soil
- NPK requirement: 20-40-20 kg/hectare
- Regions: Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Karnataka
- Sowing: October–November | Harvesting: February–March
- Key tip: Avoid excess irrigation — one or two irrigations are enough

### Potato (Aloo)
- Best soil: Sandy loam, well-drained, rich in organic matter
- NPK requirement: 150-60-100 kg/hectare
- Regions: Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Bihar, Punjab
- Sowing: October | Harvesting: January–February
- Key tip: Use certified seed tubers to avoid virus diseases

## Zaid Crops (March–June, Summer Season)

### Moong (Green Gram)
- Best soil: Loamy to sandy loam
- NPK requirement: 20-40-20 kg/hectare
- Regions: Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh
- Sowing: March–April | Harvesting: May–June

### Watermelon (Tarbooz)
- Best soil: Sandy loam
- NPK requirement: 80-60-60 kg/hectare
- Regions: Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Karnataka
- Sowing: February–March | Harvesting: May–June

## Fertilizer Application General Guidelines

### Urea (46% N)
- Apply in 2-3 split doses: basal + top dressing
- Never broadcast on dry soil — irrigate within 2 days

### DAP (18-46-0)
- Best applied as basal dose at sowing
- Place 5 cm below and 5 cm to the side of seed

### MOP / Potash (60% K2O)
- Full dose as basal application
- Important for root crops (potato, onion, carrot)

### Soil Testing
- Get soil tested every 2 years from nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)
- Soil Health Card scheme provides free testing
- Balanced fertilization based on soil test saves 20-30% fertilizer cost

## Region-Specific Recommendations

### Vidarbha (Maharashtra)
- Black soil: Cotton, Soybean, Tur (Pigeon Pea), Orange (Nagpur region)
- Focus on drip irrigation — saves 40-60% water
- Apply gypsum for sodic soils

### Punjab-Haryana Belt
- Rice-Wheat rotation (consider crop diversification)
- Stubble management: Use Happy Seeder instead of burning
- Declining water table — switch to less water-intensive crops

### Southern India (Karnataka, Tamil Nadu)
- Red soil: Ragi, Groundnut, Sunflower
- Laterite soil: Cashew, Coconut, Rubber
- Drip + mulching for horticultural crops
