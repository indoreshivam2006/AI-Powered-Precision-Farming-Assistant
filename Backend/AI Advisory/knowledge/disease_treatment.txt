# Common Crop Diseases and Treatments in India

## Rice Diseases

### Blast Disease (Magnaporthe oryzae)
- Symptoms: Spindle-shaped spots on leaves with grey center and brown border, neck rot
- Cause: Fungal — high nitrogen, humid conditions
- Treatment:
  - Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L water
  - Or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 mL/L water
  - Reduce nitrogen dose by 25% if blast is recurring
- Prevention: Use resistant varieties (Pusa Basmati 1121, Samba Mahsuri)

### Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae)
- Symptoms: Water-soaked yellow lesions on leaves starting from tip, wilting
- Cause: Bacterial — spreads through infected seeds and irrigation water
- Treatment:
  - Spray Streptocycline 500 ppm + Copper oxychloride 2.5 g/L
  - Drain excess water from field
- Prevention: Use certified seeds, avoid excess nitrogen

### Brown Plant Hopper (BPH)
- Symptoms: Hopper burn — circular patches of dried plants, honeydew on lower stem
- Cause: Insect pest — favored by excess nitrogen and close planting
- Treatment:
  - Spray Buprofezin 25% SC @ 1.6 mL/L water
  - Or Pymetrozine 50% WG @ 0.3 g/L water
  - Avoid synthetic pyrethroids (kills natural enemies)
- Prevention: Do not apply excess nitrogen, maintain proper spacing

## Wheat Diseases

### Yellow Rust / Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis)
- Symptoms: Yellow-orange pustules in stripes along leaf veins
- Cause: Fungal — cool, humid weather (10–15°C)
- Treatment:
  - Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 mL/L water (1st spray at appearance)
  - 2nd spray after 15 days if needed
- Prevention: Sow resistant varieties (HD 3226, WH 1105), avoid late sowing

### Loose Smut (Ustilago tritici)
- Symptoms: Earhead replaced by black powdery mass of spores
- Cause: Seed-borne fungal disease
- Treatment:
  - Seed treatment with Carboxin 75% WP @ 2.5 g/kg seed
  - Or Vitavax Power (Carboxin + Thiram) @ 2.5 g/kg seed
- Prevention: Use disease-free certified seeds

### Leaf Yellowing in Wheat
- Symptoms: Yellowing of lower leaves progressing upward, stunted growth
- Possible Causes:
  1. Nitrogen deficiency — Apply 30 kg urea/acre as top dressing + irrigation
  2. Iron chlorosis — Spray 0.5% ferrous sulfate + 0.25% citric acid
  3. Waterlogging — Improve drainage immediately
  4. Root rot — Check roots; if brown/black, apply Carbendazim drench

### Yellow Rust / Pila Ratua (पीला रतुआ / Yellow Rust) — Wheat
- Symptoms: Yellow pustules arranged in stripes along leaf veins, powdery yellow spores rub off on fingers
- Cause: Fungus Puccinia striiformis — favors cool humid weather (10–15°C)
- Treatment:
  - Spray Propiconazole 25% EC — 1 mL per litre of water
  - Repeat spray after 10 days if pustules persist
  - Apply in early morning when dew is present. Do not spray during rain.
- Prevention: Use resistant varieties like HD-2967, PBW-343, HD 3226. Avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer.
- States most affected: Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh

### Wheat Leaf Blight / Patti Jhulsa (पत्ती झुलसा) — Wheat
- Symptoms: Brown oval spots with yellow border on leaves, infection starts from lower older leaves and moves upward
- Cause: Fungus Alternaria triticina — spreads in warm humid conditions
- Treatment:
  - Spray Mancozeb 75% WP — 2.5 g per litre of water
  - Give 2–3 sprays at 10 day intervals starting at first sign of spots
- Prevention: Treat seeds with Thiram @ 2.5 g/kg seed before sowing. Use clean certified seed.

### Wheat Nitrogen Deficiency (गेहूं में नाइट्रोजन की कमी)
- Symptoms: Uniform yellowing of older/lower leaves first, then moves upward. No spots, no pustules, no stripes — just plain even yellowing. Stunted growth.
- This is NOT a disease — it is a nutrient deficiency.
- Treatment:
  - Apply Urea top dressing — 20–25 kg per acre
  - Irrigate the field immediately after urea application
  - For quick recovery, also do foliar spray of 2% urea solution
- How to tell apart from Yellow Rust:
  - Yellow Rust = yellow pustules/stripes on leaves, powdery spores
  - Nitrogen deficiency = plain uniform yellowing, no spots or pustules

## Cotton Diseases

### Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV)
- Symptoms: Upward curling of leaves, thickened veins, enations (outgrowths)
- Cause: Virus — transmitted by whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)
- Treatment:
  - No cure for virus; manage whitefly vector
  - Spray Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.2 g/L or Spiromesifen 240 SC @ 0.7 mL/L
- Prevention: Use tolerant varieties, remove early infected plants

### Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella)
- Symptoms: Rosette flowers, bored bolls with pink larvae inside
- Treatment:
  - Install pheromone traps (5/acre) for monitoring
  - Spray Emamectin benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L water
- Prevention: Early sowing, timely picking, destroy crop residues

## Tomato Diseases

### Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- Symptoms: Dark brown water-soaked spots on leaves, white mold underneath, fruit rot
- Treatment:
  - Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L + Metalaxyl 35% WS as preventive
  - Or Cymoxanil + Mancozeb @ 3 g/L
- Prevention: Good spacing, avoid overhead irrigation, stake plants

### Tomato Leaf Curl Virus
- Symptoms: Upward curling of leaves, yellowing, stunted plants
- Cause: Transmitted by whitefly
- Treatment:
  - Control whitefly with Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 mL/L
  - Remove and destroy infected plants
- Prevention: Use virus-resistant varieties, yellow sticky traps

## General Plant Disease Management Tips

### Yellowing of Leaves (Common Causes)
1. Nitrogen deficiency → Apply urea or foliar spray of 2% urea
2. Iron deficiency → Spray ferrous sulfate 0.5% + citric acid 0.25%
3. Magnesium deficiency → Spray magnesium sulfate 1%
4. Overwatering → Improve drainage, reduce irrigation frequency
5. Pest attack → Check underside of leaves for insects
6. Viral infection → Look for mosaic patterns, leaf curling

### Organic / IPM Approaches
- Neem oil 1500 ppm @ 5 mL/L — broad spectrum insecticide
- Trichoderma viride — soil treatment for root diseases (4 kg/acre mixed with FYM)
- Pseudomonas fluorescens — seed treatment + soil drench for wilt
- Panchagavya — organic growth promoter, spray 3% solution every 15 days
- Yellow/Blue sticky traps — for whitefly, aphids, thrips
- Cow urine spray (10%) — fungicidal and growth-promoting properties
