# Government Schemes for Indian Farmers (2024-2026)

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- What: ₹6,000 per year in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each directly to bank account
- Who: All landholding farmer families (small and marginal)
- Exclusion: Institutional landholders, income tax payers, govt employees
- How to apply:
  1. Visit pmkisan.gov.in or your nearest CSC (Common Service Centre)
  2. Provide Aadhaar number, bank account details, and land records
  3. Verification by state/district officials
- Helpline: 155261 or 011-24300606
- Status check: pmkisan.gov.in → Beneficiary Status → Enter Aadhaar number

## PM Fasal Bima Yojana (Crop Insurance)
- What: Crop insurance at very low premium — 2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi, 5% for commercial crops
- Coverage: Natural calamities, pest attacks, diseases, post-harvest losses (up to 14 days)
- How to apply:
  1. Visit bank branch or CSC before cutoff date (varies by crop/state)
  2. Provide crop sowing details, land records, bank account
  3. Premium deducted from bank account
- Claim process: Report crop loss within 72 hours through Crop Insurance App or toll-free number 14447
- Important: Non-loanee farmers can also enroll voluntarily

## Soil Health Card Scheme
- What: Free soil testing + personalized Soil Health Card every 2 years
- Benefits: Know exact NPK + micronutrient status of your soil, save on fertilizer cost
- How to get:
  1. Contact nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or Agriculture Department
  2. Or visit soilhealth.dac.gov.in
  3. Officials collect soil sample from your field
  4. Card delivered with crop-wise fertilizer recommendations
- Tip: Follow Soil Health Card recommendations — farmers save 20-30% on fertilizer costs

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- What: Short-term credit for crop production at 4% interest (with prompt repayment)
- Loan limit: Based on crop, area, and scale of finance
- Interest subvention: 7% interest → 3% subvention → effective 4% for timely repayment
- How to apply:
  1. Visit nearest bank (cooperative, RRB, or commercial bank)
  2. Provide land documents, Aadhaar, passport photo
  3. Loan sanctioned within 14 days
- Now covers: Animal husbandry and fisheries farmers too
- Helpline: Contact your bank's agriculture desk

## PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana (Irrigation)
- What: Subsidy on micro-irrigation (drip + sprinkler) systems
- Subsidy: 55% for small/marginal farmers, 45% for others
- Additional state top-up in many states (e.g., Maharashtra gives additional 25-30%)
- How to apply:
  1. Apply online at state agriculture portal or through district agriculture office
  2. Provide land details, water source, crop plan
  3. Get system installed by empaneled company
  4. Subsidy credited after installation verification
- Benefit: Saves 40-60% water, increases yield by 20-30%

## National Mission on Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA)
- What: Promotes organic farming, soil health management, water conservation
- Key components:
  - Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY) — ₹50,000/hectare for organic farming over 3 years
  - Soil Health Management — free/subsidized soil testing
  - Rainfed Area Development — integrated farming system support
- How to participate: Contact District Agriculture Officer or visit agricoop.nic.in

## e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)
- What: Online platform connecting mandis across India for transparent price discovery
- Benefits: Better prices, transparent bidding, reduced middlemen
- How to use:
  1. Register on enam.gov.in with Aadhaar + bank details
  2. Bring produce to nearest e-NAM enabled mandi
  3. Quality tested → listed online → traders bid → highest bid wins
- States covered: 23 states, 1,361+ mandis integrated
- Tip: Check prices across mandis before selling

## Pradhan Mantri Krishi Udan Yojana
- What: Subsidized air cargo for perishable agricultural products
- Benefits: Farmers in northeast, tribal, and hilly areas get transport subsidy
- Covers: Fruits, vegetables, flowers, meat, fish, dairy
- How to avail: Through airlines and airports participating in the scheme

## State-Level Schemes (Examples)

### Maharashtra
- Magel Tyala Shettale (Farm Pond Scheme) — subsidy for farm pond construction
- Nanaji Deshmukh Krishi Sanjivani Yojana — climate-resilient agriculture in Vidarbha and Marathwada
- Bhausaheb Fundkar Fruit Plantation Scheme

### Madhya Pradesh
- Bhavantar Bhugtan Yojana — price deficiency payment to farmers
- Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana — additional ₹4,000/year (on top of PM-KISAN)

### Punjab & Haryana
- Direct Benefit Transfer for fertilizer subsidy
- Crop residue management scheme — subsidized Happy Seeder, Super SMS

## Important Helplines & Resources
- Kisan Call Centre: 1800-180-1551 (toll-free, available in local languages)
- mKisan Portal: mkisan.gov.in — SMS advisories in local language
- Kisan Suvidha App: Weather, market prices, plant protection, expert advisory
- IFFCO Kisan App: Real-time mandi prices, weather, farming tips
- Agmarknet: agmarknet.gov.in — daily mandi prices across India

## Mandi Price Information
- Check live prices: agmarknet.gov.in or e-NAM portal
- Major mandis: Azadpur (Delhi), Vashi (Mumbai), Yeshwanthpur (Bangalore)
- Tip: Compare prices across 3-4 mandis before selling
- MSP (Minimum Support Price) announced by govt for 23 crops — sell at MSP or above through FCI/NAFED procurement centres
